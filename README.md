1. PRODUCT VISION

Brand: Heir of Wisdom

Category: Interactive visual reasoning and cognitive learning for children.

Primary market: United States

Primary buyer: Parents of children preparing for CogAT, OLSAT, and NNAT-style reasoning assessments, as well as parents interested in developing their child's reasoning abilities.

Child audience: Approximately 5–13 years, with the exact supported age range to be confirmed before launch.

Core philosophy:
"Wisdom is inherited. Thinking is discovered."

Supporting philosophy:
"The answer is only the beginning."

Product positioning:
Heir of Wisdom transforms reasoning practice questions into immersive, interactive visual experiences. Instead of merely marking an answer right or wrong, the platform helps children understand why an answer works, manipulate geometric objects, discover relationships, and apply the same concept to unfamiliar questions.

The parent sets the intention. The child experiences discovery. The platform adapts the journey.

The experience must be intellectually sophisticated, visually immersive, emotionally warm, and easy to use.

Do not use leaderboards, competitive rankings, XP, streak pressure, or generic gamification.
